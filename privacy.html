Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Who we are

Rage Wholesale is an Australian business that supplies bulk clothing lots to resellers.

What we collect

When you send a request through the form on this site, we collect:

  • your name and business
  • your email and phone number
  • your store name
  • where you sell and what you're after
  • roughly how many items you sell in a week
  • where you're based
  • ad and tracking details, such as which ad or search brought you to the site

Why we collect it

So we can contact you about stock and understand how your business sells.

Who handles it

The form is processed by Formspree, which passes your request on to us. We use Google Ads, Google Analytics and the Google tag to measure whether our ads lead to enquiries and how people use this site.

We may share your email address and phone number with Google, in a securely hashed form, so we can show you relevant ads and find similar businesses through Google Ads Customer Match. You can ask us to remove your details at any time by messaging us on Instagram at @ragewholesale.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information.

Access and deletion

To ask for a copy of your details or to have them deleted, message us on Instagram at @ragewholesale.
